About importing plastic building blocks set from China

US duties on plastic building blocks set are estimated by stacking the product's normal (MFN) tariff with the additional layers that apply to goods from China. In the current 2026 schedule, Toys & holiday gifts carries an MFN rate of 0%, plus a Section 301 China layer and a forced-labor/security base where applicable.

On top of duty, US Customs charges a Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) of 0.3% (min $33.58, max $651.5) and, for ocean freight, a Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF) of 0.1%. From 1 October 2026 the MPF limits rise to $34.58/$670.86 while the rate is unchanged.

Confirm the exact 10-digit HTS code in the official USITC HTS search.

Possible change: A US–China agreement announced 2026-09-27 could lower rates on this category, but it is not yet in force — do not plan around it until implemented.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 US duty rate on plastic building blocks set from China?

What does it cost to land $1,000 of plastic building blocks set from China by ocean?

Are MPF and HMF included in the duty rate?

No. MPF (0.3%, capped $33.58–$651.5) and HMF (0.1%, ocean only) are charged on top of duty.
