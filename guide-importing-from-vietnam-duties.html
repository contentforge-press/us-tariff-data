Importing from Vietnam: Duties & Fees

Updated 2026-10-01 · planning estimates

Vietnam-origin goods pay the MFN rate but no Section 301 China tariff. In the current schedule a security/forced-labor base of 12.5% applies to many non-exempt categories, plus Section 232 where relevant, and MPF/HMF on top.

See the Vietnam rate-by-product guide for category-by-category figures.
