About importing smartphone from China

US duties on smartphone are estimated by stacking the product's normal (MFN) tariff with the additional layers that apply to goods from China. In the current 2026 schedule, Consumer electronics carries an MFN rate of 0%, plus a Section 301 China layer and a forced-labor/security base where applicable.

On top of duty, US Customs charges a Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) of 0.3% (min $33.58, max $651.5) and, for ocean freight, a Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF) of 0.1%. From 1 October 2026 the MPF limits rise to $34.58/$670.86 while the rate is unchanged.
