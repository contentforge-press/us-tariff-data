About importing webcam from South Korea

US duties on webcam are estimated by stacking the product's normal (MFN) tariff with the additional layers that apply to goods from South Korea. In the current 2026 schedule, Consumer electronics carries an MFN rate of 0%; Section 301 China tariffs do not apply to goods of South Korea origin, though a 12.5% security base may.

On top of duty, US Customs charges a Merchandise Processing Fee (MPF) of 0.3% (min $34.58, max $670.86, effective 1 October 2026) and, for ocean freight, a Harbor Maintenance Fee (HMF) of 0.1%. The ad-valorem MPF rate is unchanged; only the minimum and maximum rose for fiscal year 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 US duty rate on webcam from South Korea?

The estimated total stacked rate is 12.5% (MFN 0% plus any applicable security/Section 301 layers), before MPF and HMF.

What does it cost to land $1,000 of webcam from South Korea by ocean?

Customs duty is about $125.00, MPF $34.58, HMF $1.25, giving a landed cost around $1,160.83 before freight and broker fees.

Are MPF and HMF included in the duty rate?

No. MPF (0.3%, capped $34.58–$670.86) and HMF (0.1%, ocean only) are charged on top of duty.
