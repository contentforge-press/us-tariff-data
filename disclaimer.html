What you should know

  • Tariff rates, surcharges and fee thresholds change frequently — sometimes with immediate effect. The data here reflects the environment as of 2026-10-01 and may already be outdated for your shipment.
  • Classification (the correct 10-digit HTS code) is the single biggest driver of the final rate. A wrong classification can change duty by double-digit percentage points; always confirm with a licensed customs broker.
  • Landed-cost figures include assumptions about freight, insurance and exchange rates that are stated on each page; your actual costs will differ.
  • Section 301 and 232 measures carry specific scope rules, exclusions and reporting requirements that are summarized only at a high level here.
