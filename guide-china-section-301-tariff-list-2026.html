Section 301 China Tariff List 2026

Updated 2026-10-01 · planning estimates

Section 301 tariffs on Chinese goods currently add 25% on top of MFN rates for most product categories. Some categories have specific overrides:

CategorySection 301 rateTotal from China
General merchandise25%41%
Consumer electronics25%37.5%
Apparel & clothing25%54%
Steel / aluminum articles25%75%
Electric vehicles100%115.0%
Solar panels50%62.5%
Lithium-ion batteries25%40.9%
Semiconductors50%87.5%

A possible US–China tariff-cut framework covering ~$30B was announced on 2026-09-27, targeting toys, appliances, baby products and kitchen/bath items. This is not yet in force. Do not treat pending cuts as current rates.
