HTS Chapter 69: Ceramic products

Updated 2026-10-01 · planning estimate · verify the 10-digit code in the official HTS

The Harmonized Tariff Schedule groups related goods into chapters. Chapter 69 covers Ceramic products. The chapter only locates the right part of the schedule — the exact duty depends on the heading, subheading and the country layers that stack on top, so two goods in the same chapter can carry quite different rates.
